# Airport Check-in and Security Timing Recommendations

Arriving too late is a common reason travelers miss flights. Recommended
buffer times depend on route type, airport size, and whether you have bags.

**Domestic flights (same country):** many airlines suggest arriving **90
minutes** before departure. If you only have cabin bags and already checked in
online, 60–75 minutes can work at smaller airports—but busy morning peaks and
extra security screening can erase that margin.

**International flights:** plan for **2.5–3 hours** before departure. You may
need passport control, longer security lines, and sometimes document checks at
the gate. Some destinations require earlier presence for visa or exit formalities.

**Check-in deadlines** (when the airline stops accepting passengers) are
usually 45–60 minutes before domestic departures and 60–90 minutes before
international ones. Bag drop often closes at the same time. Online check-in
typically opens 24–48 hours beforehand and lets you skip the desk if you are
traveling light.

**Security and boarding:** after security, allow time to reach a distant gate,
especially in large hubs. Boarding often starts 30–50 minutes before departure
and can close 10–15 minutes before pushback. Families, mobility assistance, and
premium cabins may board earlier.

Build extra time for first visits to an airport, connecting from another
flight, traveling with children, or peak holiday periods. Missing the bag-drop
or boarding cutoff usually means buying a new ticket, so treat airline
deadlines as hard limits rather than suggestions.
